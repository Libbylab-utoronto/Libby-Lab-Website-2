Maria completed her BSc in Anatomy and Cell Biology at McGill. Her interest in developmental biology and healthcare led her to pursue a MHSc in Clinical Embryology at the University of Toronto. Now returning to research, Maria is interested in investigating the cellular and molecular mechanisms underlying neural tube development and morphogenesis. Outside the lab, Maria enjoys discovering new coffee spots, reading and making memories with family and friends.
