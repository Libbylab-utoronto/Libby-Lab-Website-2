Shreyas Sridhar Postdoctoral Researcher University of Toronto

Shreyas is a molecular cell biologist with expertise in genomics and computational analysis. He did his PhD with Kaustuv Sanyal at JNCASR, Bangalore, India, where he studied the kinetochore, the machinery that segregates chromosomes, in the fungal pathogen Cryptococcus neoformans, and discovered bridgin, a new family of kinetochore proteins. He then did his postdoctoral research with Tatsuo Fukagawa at the University of Osaka, Japan, supported by two early-career grants from the Japan Society for the Promotion of Science. There he investigated how vertebrate cells maintain their kinetochores and started new projects on kinetochore evolution that used genome assembly and comparative genomics.

His interest in how cells adapt their core machinery as they take on new identities brought him to the Libby Lab, where he combines human embryonic stem cells with the chick embryo as an in vivo model to study the origin and specification of trunk neural crest cells. Outside the lab, he enjoys travelling and spending time with family and friends.
