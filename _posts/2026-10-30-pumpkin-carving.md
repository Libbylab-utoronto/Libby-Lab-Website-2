Pumpkin Carving at the Donnelly Centre: A Gourd Time Was Had by All!

What a turnout for this year’s Donnelly Centre Pumpkin Carving! Scalpels were swapped for carving knives, pipettes for pumpkin scoops, and the creativity was off the charts. A huge thank you to the DCGSA for providing the pumpkins, and to everyone who came with big ideas and the follow-through to bring them to life. Some of these jack-o’-lanterns deserve their own publication!
